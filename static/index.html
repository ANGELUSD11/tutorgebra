What is the API Key?

TutorGebra needs a "brain" to read your exercises and convert them into geometric code. That brain is Gemini 2.5 Flash, Google's AI. To connect to it for free, you need a personal key (API Key).

100% Secure: Your API key acts as a direct bridge between you and Google. We never log, collect, or store your key on our servers.

Steps to get your key (It's free!):

  1. Go to Google AI Studio.
  2. Log in with your Google account.
  3. Click the blue "Create API key" button.
  4. Copy the long text starting with AIzaSy... and paste it into TutorGebra.
