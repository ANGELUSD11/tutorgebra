What is the API Key?

TutorGebra needs a "brain" to read your exercises and convert them into geometric code. We use OpenRouter to route your requests to the best free AI models available (like Gemini). If the server key is exhausted, you can use your own personal key.

100% Secure: Your API key acts as a direct bridge between you and OpenRouter. We never log, collect, or store your key on our servers.

Steps to get your key (It's free!):

  1. Go to OpenRouter.ai.
  2. Log in with your Google or GitHub account.
  3. Click the "Create Key" button.
  4. Copy the generated code and paste it into TutorGebra.
